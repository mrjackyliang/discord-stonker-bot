Sign in to the control panel and find your way around, from the server menu and page navigation to the Overview page.

## Why Use the Control Panel?

1. Puts the bot's controls, features, logs, and accounts on one browser page, so you don't edit files or restart containers to change settings.
2. Builds a form for each feature with channel and role pickers, so you choose names instead of typing IDs.
3. Applies saved settings by reconnecting the bot on its own, so changes take effect without a container restart.
4. Encrypts every token and secret you paste, so they're never shown again after you save them.

## Sign In

Open the control panel at its address, such as `http://<docker-host>:22604/`, and the **Sign in** screen appears whenever you're signed out. Before the first account exists, the panel shows **Set up your account** instead, as described in [First-Run Setup](/docs/getting-started/first-run/#create-the-owner-account).

### With a Password

1. Enter your **Username** and **Password**. The username isn't case-sensitive.
2. Select **Sign in**.

A wrong username, a wrong password, and a suspended account all show "The username or password is incorrect."

### With an Authenticator or Recovery Code

If you turned on an <Terminology title="App that generates six-digit sign-in codes" to="/docs/reference/terminology#authenticator-app">authenticator app</Terminology>, **Enter your code** appears after the password step.

1. Enter the 6-digit code from the app, or one of your <Terminology title="One-time code used instead of an authenticator code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#recovery-code">recovery codes</Terminology>.
2. Select **Verify**, or select **Start over** to go back to the password step.

- **Time limit** — You have 5 minutes after the password step, and 5 wrong codes end the attempt with "Too many wrong codes. Enter your password again."
- **One use each** — Each authenticator code and each <Terminology title="One-time code used instead of an authenticator code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#recovery-code">recovery code</Terminology> works once. A <Terminology title="One-time code used instead of an authenticator code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#recovery-code">recovery code</Terminology> works with or without its dash, in any letter case.

### With a Passkey

1. Select **Sign in with a <Terminology title="Device sign-in that replaces your password and code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#passkey">passkey</Terminology>**.
2. Confirm with your device or password manager when the browser asks.

A <Terminology title="Device sign-in that replaces your password and code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#passkey">passkey</Terminology> replaces the username, the password, and the authenticator code. The button appears only where the browser can use <Terminology title="Device sign-in that replaces your password and code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#passkey">passkeys</Terminology>, as described in [Remote Access](/docs/operations/remote-access/#passkeys-and-the-address).

### Failed Attempts

- **Shared limit** — Wrong passwords, wrong codes, rejected <Terminology title="Device sign-in that replaces your password and code" to="/docs/reference/terminology#passkey">passkeys</Terminology>, and wrong password confirmations from one address share a limit of 10 failures in 15 minutes.
- **Over the limit** — Sign-in from that address shows "Too many sign-in attempts. Try again in 15 minutes." until the oldest failure is 15 minutes old.
- **Clearing the count** — A successful sign-in clears the count for that address.
- **Alert** — Reaching the limit sends a **Sign-in locked** alert to the [notification](/docs/control-panel/notifications/) <Terminology title="One place alerts go, such as a chat or topic" to="/docs/reference/terminology#notification-destination">destinations</Terminology> that chose it.

## Sessions

- **Length** — A <Terminology title="24-hour sign-in kept in a browser cookie" to="/docs/reference/terminology#browser-session">browser session</Terminology> lasts 24 hours from sign-in, and then the panel returns to the sign-in screen.
- **Restarts** — Sessions are stored in the database, so restarting or updating the container doesn't sign you out.
- **Sign out** — **Sign out** in the account menu ends your session right away, and changing your password signs out your other devices.

## Navigation

The sidebar holds the server pages and Settings pages, while account security stays in the account menu.

- **Server menu** — At the top, shows the selected server and switches between the servers your account can open. It also holds **Add a server**, and a server the bot has left shows **Bot removed**.
- **Overview, Features, Logs** — The main server pages. **Overview** and **Features** use the selected server, while **Logs** shows every entry whichever server you pick. Selecting **Web applications** on **Features** opens the selected server's **Invite page** designer.
- **Settings** — **Connection**, **Backup**, **Users**, **Access tokens**, and **Notifications**. **Backup** and **Users** appear only for the <Terminology title="The single account made at setup, with full access" to="/docs/reference/terminology#owner">Owner</Terminology> and <Terminology title="Role that can change everything, including users" to="/docs/reference/terminology#administrator">Administrators</Terminology>, and **Notifications** also appears for <Terminology title="Role that changes features and runs the bot" to="/docs/reference/terminology#operator">Operators</Terminology>.
- **Bot status** — The card at the bottom shows the <Terminology title="The bot's last reported connection state" to="/docs/reference/terminology#bot-status">bot status</Terminology>, and selecting it opens **Connection**.
- **Account menu** — Shows your username and <Terminology title="Owner, Administrator, Operator, or Viewer access level" to="/docs/reference/terminology#panel-role">panel role</Terminology>, with **Appearance**, **Sign-in and security**, and **Sign out**.

### Page Addresses

| Page                     | Address                             |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Overview**             | `/overview` (`/` also opens it)     |
| **Features**             | `/features`                         |
| A feature's own page     | `/features/:feature-id`             |
| **Invite page**          | `/features/web-applications/invite` |
| **Logs**                 | `/logs`                             |
| **Connection**           | `/connection`                       |
| **Backup**               | `/backup`                           |
| **Users**                | `/users`                            |
| **Access tokens**        | `/access-tokens`                    |
| **Notifications**        | `/notifications`                    |
| **Sign-in and security** | `/account`                          |

Browser **Back** and **Forward**, refresh, and bookmarks reopen the page at its address. If the panel asks you to **Sign in**, it opens the requested page after sign-in; an unknown address opens **Overview** at `/overview`.

The old `/import` address opens **Backup** at `/backup`.

## The Overview Page

The Overview page summarizes the bot, the services, the selected server, and your account.

- **Bot** — The <Terminology title="The bot's last reported connection state" to="/docs/reference/terminology#bot-status">bot status</Terminology>, whether the <Terminology title="Secret credential the bot uses to sign in" to="/docs/reference/terminology#bot-token">Bot Token</Terminology> is saved, the bot's Discord account, the Application ID, and how many servers it joined.
- **Problems** — Below the bot details, the panel shows how to fix a failed start, and lists any <Terminology title="Errors that kept features off at the last connect" to="/docs/reference/terminology#configuration-issues">configuration issues</Terminology> from the last connection.
- **Services** — The status of the **Bot process**, the **Control panel**, and **Scheduled jobs**, such as **Ready**. A service that hasn't sent a <Terminology title="Latest status a service reported to the database" to="/docs/reference/terminology#heartbeat">heartbeat</Terminology> for 90 seconds shows **Not responding** and when its last one arrived.
- **Server** — The selected server's <Terminology title="Number Discord uses to identify a server" to="/docs/reference/terminology#server-id">Server ID</Terminology> with a copy button, the number of features turned on, whether the bot is still in the server, and how many servers you can open.
- **Your account** — Your username, <Terminology title="Owner, Administrator, Operator, or Viewer access level" to="/docs/reference/terminology#panel-role">panel role</Terminology>, and <Terminology title="Which Discord servers a panel account may open" to="/docs/reference/terminology#server-access">server access</Terminology>.

[Export and Import](/docs/control-panel/export-and-import/) covers **Backup**, [Users and Security](/docs/control-panel/users-and-security/) explains what each <Terminology title="Owner, Administrator, Operator, or Viewer access level" to="/docs/reference/terminology#panel-role">panel role</Terminology> can change, [Access Tokens and API](/docs/control-panel/access-tokens/) covers tokens, and [Notifications](/docs/control-panel/notifications/) covers alerts.

## Appearance

Choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** under **Appearance** in the account menu. **System** follows your device's setting, and the choice is saved in this browser only.
